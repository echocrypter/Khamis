Maison Philosophy

The Architecture of Silence

KHAMIS was founded on the radical premise that modern clothing is architectural volume interacting with time, daylight, and physical stride. We reject the ephemeral cycles of disposable fashion in favor of monolithic permanence.

Every piece in our Haute Couture and Ready-to-Wear collections is engineered in our Parisian atelier and hand-finished by master craftspeople in Geneva and Piedmont. We source exclusively uncompromised raw materials: double-faced cashmere woven in northern Italy, high-density raw Japanese wool faille, vegetable-tanned French calfskin, and 18-karat recycled golds.

“Form is not decorated onto the silhouette; form is the silhouette itself, sculpted with mathematical discipline and fluid grace.”

All KHAMIS garments are produced in strict numbered limited runs to prevent overproduction and preserve rarity.
